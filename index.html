TreeVision
Urban Tree Mapping

TreeVision helps cities, NGOs, farmers, and researchers register, monitor, and manage urban and fruit trees across their entire life cycle from planting and growth tracking to harvest, advisory services, and decision-making. Combining high-precision GPS field data collection, interactive mapping, and powerful analytics, the app turns community action into actionable ecological data for a greener, more resilient future.

Urban & Fruit Tree Value Chain

TreeVision digitizes the full life cycle of urban and fruit trees capturing and managing key information from planting, production, and distribution through to growth, maintenance, fruit production, harvesting, sales, and advisory services. The platform strengthens linkages among farmers, private sector actors, extension service providers, and relevant government institutions.

Tree Life Cycle Digitalization

Capture, monitor, and manage key information throughout the urban and fruit tree life cycle from planting, production and distribution to growth, maintenance, fruit production, harvesting, sales, and advisory services.

Value Chain Integration

Digitalize key processes and information flows across the tree value chain, strengthening linkages among farmers, private sector actors, extension service providers, and relevant government institutions.

Training & Onboarding

Support training and onboarding of relevant users farmers, private sector actors, and government stakeholders to ensure effective adoption and sustainable use of the system.

Data Collection & Management

Establish user-friendly mechanisms for collecting, storing, updating, and managing reliable and timely data on urban and fruit trees, farmers, production, harvesting, and marketing. These tools focus on precision mapping, visual documentation, and efficient data handling in any environment.

Interactive Mapping

GPS-based visualization of farms or individual trees and planting areas, with filtering and viewing of trees by location, species, farmer, and status including mapping of restoration and agroforestry activities.

Visual Documentation

Support for photo updates to track health and growth over time.

Optimized Data Usage

Support for background photo uploads when on Wi-Fi, preserving battery and mobile data while working in the field.

Tree Bookmarks

Ability to track specific trees of interest for easier follow-up and monitoring.

Custom Imagery

Support for custom map tiles, enabling the use of high-resolution internal aerial imagery for precise local mapping.

Street View Integration

Support for 360° street-level imagery, including specialized tools to triangulate and verify tree positions directly from the imagery.

Accessible Technology

Create accurate maps using standard consumer-grade devices. The platform is designed to produce professional results without requiring expensive surveying equipment.

Efficient Survey Modes

Choose from various survey modes tailored to your available tools. The platform offers optimized workflows that adapt to your equipment and team size to maximize productivity in the field.

Mobile-First Collection

Use your existing smartphone to collect all data in the field through a specialized mobile interface, then switch to a laptop for in-depth data analysis and management.

Analytics & Decision Support

Integrate data analytics and reporting functionalities to generate actionable information for farmers, value chain actors, and decision-makers supporting improved planning, productivity, transparency, and evidence-based decision-making.

Powerful Search

Built-in search functionality to quickly find trees based on species, address, farmer, or any other collected attribute across your entire inventory.

AI & Decision Support

AI-powered insights and native Model Context Protocol (MCP) support, enabling intelligent agents to perform complex queries, generate advisory reports, and provide deep insights into your tree data.

Comprehensive Analytics

Detailed reporting including biodiversity indexing, species leaderboards, harvest statistics, and coverage analytics to support evidence-based planning and transparent reporting to stakeholders.

OpenStreetMap Integration

Bidirectional data exchange with OSM, allowing for pulling existing tree data and pushing local updates back to the global map.

Performance

A Rust-based backend capable of managing large datasets with minimal infrastructure requirements.

Data Portability

Export your data in standard GeoJSON format for use in QGIS, ArcGIS, or custom web APIs. Your data remains accessible and compatible with industry-standard GIS tools.

Community

TreeVision is built to facilitate collaboration between volunteers, administrators, and the public. These tools help coordinate field work, manage contributions, and engage the wider community in urban forestry efforts.

Communication Tools

Integrated commenting system to facilitate discussion between team members and administrators about specific trees.

Citizen Feedback

Integration with Telegram for reporting remarkable sightings, new plantings, or maintenance needs by volunteers and the public.

Access Control

Granular permissions to manage contributions from volunteers and staff, ensuring data integrity while encouraging wide participation.

Public Engagement

A user-friendly public interface that allows citizens to explore their urban forest, learn about local species, and follow greening progress.
